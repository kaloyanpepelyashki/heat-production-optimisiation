# High-Level Requirements for Heat Production Optimization System

## Business Objectives
1. **Increase Efficiency**: Optimize heat production processes to minimize costs and maximize output efficiency.
2. **Cost Reduction**: Lower operational costs by improving energy utilization and reducing downtime.
3. **Sustainability**: Ensure the system supports eco-friendly practices by minimizing emissions and promoting use of renewable energy sources.
4. **User Satisfaction**: Provide end-users with reliable, heat production that meets demand effectively and possibly reduces prices.

## High-Level Requirements
1. **User Authentication**: The system must implement secure user authentication to protect sensitive data and ensure authorized access.
2. **Data Integration**: Ability to integrate with existing systems (e.g., supply chain, energy management) - data flow and real-time monitoring.
3. **Automated Reporting**: The system should generate automated reports on efficiency, costs, and emissions for management review.
4. **Real-Time Monitoring**: Implement monitoring tools to track production metrics in real-time and provide alerts for any deviations from expected performance.
5. **Optimization Algorithms**: Develop algorithms to analyze production data and recommend optimization strategies based on current and forecasted demand.
6. **Scalability**: The system should be designed to scale, allowing for expansion of operations and integration of future technologies.
7. **User Interface**: A user-friendly interface is necessary for operators to easily navigate the system and obtain the information needed for decision-making.
8. **Regulatory Compliance**: Ensure the system complies with all relevant regulations regarding heat production and environmental standards.
